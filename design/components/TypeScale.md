# TypeScale

Two Google Fonts: **Fraunces** (display, a soft bookish serif) and **Nunito Sans** (UI, warm and round).

| Style | Font | Size / line | Weight | Tracking | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hero-count | Fraunces | 96 / 96 | 300 | −0.03em | "128" |
| hero-unit | Fraunces | 22 | 400 | 0 | "books" next to the count, ink-2 |
| app-title | Fraunces | 36 / 42 | 500 ("BB's"), 400 italic ("Books") | −0.02em | App title |
| card-title | Fraunces | 21 / 1.15 | 400 | 0 | Reading card book title |
| cover-title | Fraunces | 17 / 1.05 | 400 | 0 | Cover tiles (12 / 1.05 on the small card cover) |
| chip | Nunito Sans | 14 | 700 | 0 | Chip labels |
| body | Nunito Sans | 13 | 600–700 | 0 | Authors, short text |
| eyebrow | Nunito Sans | 12 | 700, uppercase | 0.04em | Section labels, with a 7px cobalt dot |
| caption | Nunito Sans | 12 | 600–700 | 0 | Progress and meta |
| cover-author | Nunito Sans | 9 | 700, uppercase | 0.14em | Author on a cover tile |

**Pairing rules:** Fraunces for what a reader would say out loud (name, count, titles), Nunito Sans for everything else. Only "Books" is italic. Fraunces is never bold, and Nunito Sans is never lighter than 600 at these sizes.
