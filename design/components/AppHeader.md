# AppHeader

The app title "BB's *Books*" on the left and a round profile button on the right, sitting on the white top zone.

**Specs.** Title Fraunces 36/42, weight 500, −0.02em, ink. "Books" is weight 400 italic. Title top at y = 50, 20px from the left. Profile button 40px circle, `tint` fill, 1px `tint-border`, 22px outline user icon in `accent`, top at y = 54, 20px from the right.

**States.** Default, pressed (tint deepens slightly), focus (2px cobalt ring, 2px offset), and with a photo (photo fills the circle).

**Do:** keep it on white, keep the italic on "Books" only, label the button "Profile" for screen readers.
**Don't:** bold the title, swap in a logo, use glass for the profile button (glass looks grey on white).
